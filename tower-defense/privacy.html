Sky Kings privacy policy

MVP Studio, last updated November 25, 2026.

Sky Kings has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We don't sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and saved matches are stored only on your device.

What is sent over the internet, and only when you choose to

  • Feedback: if you tap Send on the feedback form, the text you typed and a short summary of your last match (faction, round, result) go to the development team. Nothing identifies you unless you type it.
  • Online matches: if you play online, your moves and the room code you join pass through our game server so the other players' games stay in sync. The server keeps nothing after the match ends.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date.
